## Overall assessment

All 20 images are separate single-asset references with premium tactile materials and uniform light cool gray backgrounds. None contains a character, mannequin, stand, text label or multi-view sheet. Left/right orientations and front-only ornament placement are usable and consistent. The previously reported Navigator squat profile and Explorer extra side rib have been addressed.

The residual issues are cross-view construction and camera-registration details typical of separately generated concept references. The Navigator crown/rim registration and Aviator buckle count/position deserve explicit attention during 3D reconstruction. This review does not claim exact CAD-style four-view consistency.

The separately authored SignalDiadem is outside the 20-image independent scope. Its two side views retain a documented mild projection limitation in its generation.json.
