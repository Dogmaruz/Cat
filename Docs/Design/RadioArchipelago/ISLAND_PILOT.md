# First playable island

Scene: `DancingCatsClone/Assets/RadioArchipelago/Scenes/EmeraldIslandPilot.unity`.
In Unity, enter Play Mode and press **ИГРАТЬ**. Music is the existing **Always Remember Meow**, 157.153 seconds. An isolated Android test build is now authorized for this island and song only.

The first V4 island, **Изумрудное ущелье**, uses its canonical 514.926 m winding closed road. The current pilot maps chart distance with a 1.18 world-distance scale for faster movement, while retaining the song BPM and DSP timings. Tiles ahead are shown in a bounded distance window so future laps do not overlap. Left/right movement is measured across the local road tangent. Camera position and heading follow the road with a forward look and smooth turning.

The existing DSP clock, physical chart-space collisions, hold durations, glides, big jumps, sound triggers and courier animation remain authoritative. `IslandStagePresentation` owns a separate world view, maps the courier and tile visuals, and owns their cleanup. It does not change chart time or teleport the logical actor. The pilot chart is a separate asset with roughly 15% wider lateral phrasing, constrained by the existing flight-time reachability policy. The source chart and MP3 remain unchanged.

Files:

- `Assets/RadioArchipelago/Runtime/World/IslandRoute.cs`: immutable, allocation-free sampled closed route and local tangent frame.
- `IslandStageDefinition.cs`: scene content, chart and camera settings.
- `IslandStagePresentation.cs`: curved tile visuals, actor projection and camera.
- `IslandPilotEnvironment.cs`: scene-owned rendering quality, restored when the scene unloads; preview song selection does not change the saved selection.
- `Assets/RadioArchipelago/Editor/IslandPilotJobs.cs`: deterministic native asset/scene authoring and checks.
- `IslandChartAuthoring.cs`: timing-preserving pilot chart derivative.
- `Assets/RadioArchipelago/Art/IslandSurface.shader`: lit vertex colors and stone detail, compatible with Forward+ and URP light layers.
- `IslandParticleProjection.cs`: pooled projection of existing native particle, trail and effect meshes; preserves original effect assets.

The source BLEND remains under `ArtSource/RadioArchipelago/Islands/V4/Output/01_emerald_ravine`. Runtime FBX is a derivative with reduced foliage/branch geometry and spatial mesh batches. The full procedural Blender materials are approximated with vertex colors and a fixed stone texture in Unity. This is a desktop Editor pilot, not a mobile performance certification.

Lighting uses warm sunlight with soft cascaded shadows, cool sky ambient fill and a procedural sky. Bloom, screen post-processing, platform emission and SSAO are disabled. SSAO is disabled on a pilot-owned renderer copy, leaving the original renderer untouched. The pilot uses its own render-pipeline asset; the project's existing mobile asset is not edited by these jobs. Native FBX conversion is checked with three coordinate anchors, and the island wrapper corrects its horizontal handedness to match the canonical route.

The installed Editor is 6000.5.5f1; the project was opened from 6000.5.4f1 after settings/package backups in `ArtSource/RadioArchipelago/IslandPilot/BeforeEditorPatch`. Unity's automatic dependency upgrades were reverted to the original pinned manifest/lock.

Tiles now have a resting top at 1.0 m and thickness 0.30 m. The visible gap below them is at least 0.564 m over the highest cobble. A short hit presses the platform down 0.15 m, rebounds and shrinks away over 0.52 s; held supports depress 0.08 m until released. The courier follows that support offset visually without changing musical/physical chart time.

The V2 roadway contains 14,098 individual rounded 3D cobbles of six shape families, with broad size variation, full orientation variation and irregular compact placement. Final native geometry is 4,984,072 triangles in 329 renderer batches. Source art and the first paving version are preserved.

The automatic quality policy starts the Editor in Rich, mobile in Balanced, and devices reporting at most 3 GB RAM in Economy. Sustained poor frame time lowers quality after warmup. Economy reduces particle budget, shadow resolution/cascades, render scale and far clip. Post-processing stays off in every tier, including after scene/UI callbacks. Runtime render-pipeline/profile clones prevent settings from leaking into normal game assets. Physical phones and thermal throttling remain untested.

The Hold road correction reuses original native body geometry, atlas value detail, line, points and effects. Its body now matches the mint round platforms. The broad Hold_Light overlay uses a view-only copy of the native alpha-blended line shader, white RGB and 24% of its animated source Alpha. This is necessary because several original Hold_Light variants use opaque shaders that ignore color alpha. The central Hold_Line keeps native Alpha and end fades, with white RGB. Both shaders have explicit serialized references in IslandStageDefinition so they are included in a Player. Fifty pilot-readable native mesh copies permit UV-preserving subdivision/deformation; originals remain unchanged. Hidden logical HoldExit/FollowEnd events produce no surrogate discs. Shared stage visibility and unwrapped chart expiry remove consumed strips before later laps.

Courier visual scale is 0.64 instead of 0.47 (36% larger); the logical collision radius and musical timings stay unchanged. Scale is reapplied after courier binding during preparation because the initial role is created before a song is selected. The QA first reproduced the previous 0.47 cold-launch scale, then verifies actual scale in the prepared menu and during play. Finish coin views reuse the native coin mesh/material/animated pose, map into the island route, and hide when the authoritative native collider is collected. They create no duplicate colliders or rewards.

Lateral smoothing now uses `1 - pow(0.75, deltaTime * 60)` instead of a fixed gain of 0.25 per rendered frame. This preserves the 60 Hz response while preventing slower steering at 20/30 Hz. `IslandSteeringChecks` verifies equal response after 100 ms at 20, 30, 60 and 120 Hz, native 60 Hz gain, zero elapsed time and lane limits.

The first 28 October camera feedback is recorded in `IslandPilotReview/CameraFeedback20261028/README.md`. The original camera reproduced clipping on a 590×1280 phone viewport. The initial correction used a post-damping frustum fit with animated courier bounds and the next landing rim; that strategy was subsequently replaced as described below. View-local scenery cutaway keeps foreground rocks/foliage from obscuring the courier or next tile. Ground, other cameras and shadows retain their normal appearance. Exact native FX geometry caching retains animated materials, visibility, skins and spring changes while avoiding unchanged per-vertex deformation/uploads. Review `20261028-085030-405` established containment across 9,541 observed frames but did not measure temporal camera motion. Mean Editor effect projection CPU fell from 6.610 to 1.836 ms (geometry 5.628 to 0.837 ms); these instrumented desktop measurements do not certify phone FPS. Actual before/after finish frames and a URP occluder pixel test accompany the report. The existing v2 APK has not been rebuilt.

The subsequent jitter report supersedes that camera strategy: see `IslandPilotReview/CameraJitter20261028/README.md`. The post-damping fit reacted to padded animated culling bounds, producing up to 4.174 m of camera motion in a stationary-road regression. Camera composition now uses a fixed route-only swept envelope and an 18.4 m road horizon, fits the desired pose before damping, and excludes jump height, lateral input, animated bounds and discrete landing selection from its target. Animated bounds remain only in scenery cutaway. Independent smoothed world-space state also cancels movement inherited from the native railway during its finish animation. Stability checks cover 360 courier poses (zero camera drift) and 60 parent-transport steps (less than 1 micrometre drift). Full Lumen review `20261028-120341-823` passed across 9,524 observed frames, with 9,503 consecutive camera-damping checks reporting zero position/rotation residual including the finish; courier/nearest-landing containment remained valid. The camera changes do not modify chart/music timing, native steering or courier animation; no APK is rebuilt.
